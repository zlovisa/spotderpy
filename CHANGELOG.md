# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10

First public release.

- Copy a Spotify song link and the matching YouTube video opens.
- Copy a playlist or album link to get a page with every song's video, a **Play all** button and **Copy all links**.
- Copy many song links at once (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C in Spotify) for playlists over 100 songs.
- Main window: paste a link, compare the top matches with thumbnails, and browse your history.
- Settings: start at sign-in, open right away or show the matches first, music video vs. audio-only, notifications, results folder.
- Smarter matching: song length, official channels, and the right version (instrumental, live, remix…).
- Automatic update checks, a Windows installer and a portable version.
